# H-1B Transfer and PERM Restart Tools — M21

Milestone: M21 — H-1B transfer and PERM restart timeline estimator

## Routes

- `/tools/h1b-transfer-risk-checklist`
- `/tools/perm-restart-timeline-estimator`

Both pages are implemented as indexable tool pages because they provide original Chinese checklists, official-source context, visible disclaimers, internal links to company data, and no sensitive personal data collection.

## Privacy Boundary

The tools only accept generic scenario options:

- H-1B transfer scenario.
- H-1B start-timing preference.
- PERM restart scenario.
- Current generic PERM stage.
- Public company-data focus.

They do not ask for:

- I-94 dates.
- Receipt numbers.
- Priority dates.
- Salary.
- Passport, address, family, or immigration history details.
- Employer or individual names.

## H-1B Transfer Checklist

The checklist groups user-visible guidance into:

- Petition and Form I-129 filing questions.
- LCA, worksite, remote-work, and wage-context questions.
- Start-timing questions that distinguish filed, receipt, approval, and employer policy.
- Public company-data context from H-1B/LCA and PERM routes.

The page does not decide whether a user can start work, whether a petition is approvable, or whether a specific portability scenario applies.

## PERM Restart Timeline

The PERM tool uses a relative process timeline:

1. Confirm employer and permanent job opportunity.
2. Prevailing wage / PWD background.
3. Recruitment preparation.
4. ETA-9089 / PERM filing.
5. PERM certified and post-PERM nodes such as I-140 and priority-date planning.

It does not calculate real dates, decide whether an existing PERM can continue, or provide job-change legal advice.
